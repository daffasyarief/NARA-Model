# NARA
**NARA** is an experimental multimodal AI model developed by **TURINE.AI** for multihazard forecasting and environmental risk intelligence.
NARA explores the use of artificial intelligence to learn complex **spatiotemporal patterns** from heterogeneous environmental and disaster-related data.
The model is designed to combine historical information, geospatial data, satellite observations, and real-time sensor data to estimate evolving hazard risks.
NARA is intended to support research across multiple environmental hazards, including earthquakes, tsunamis, floods, landslides, wildfires, and extreme weather.

## Research Areas
* Multihazard forecasting
* Spatiotemporal modeling
* Multimodal data fusion
* Probabilistic risk estimation
* Real-time risk assessment
* Geospatial intelligence
* Disaster mitigation

## Data Sources
NARA is designed to work with heterogeneous data sources such as:
* Seismic and geophysical observations
* Meteorological and climate data
* Rainfall and hydrological measurements
* Ocean and river observations
* Satellite imagery
* Geospatial datasets
* Real-time sensor streams

## Status
**Experimental / Research**
NARA is currently under active development. Model architecture, datasets, training strategies, and supported hazard domains may evolve during development.
NARA is intended for research and decision-support applications and does not replace official disaster monitoring or early-warning systems.
